3. Scoped learning and bounded evidence - IMPLEMENTED; LIVE ACCEPTANCE PENDING

   Implemented: immutable task/profile provenance scoping, latest-decision
   deduplication, bounded context, conservative selected-variant attribution for
   metadata examples, and stored learning-record IDs for metadata/discovery.
   Unknown-origin history remains stored and is excluded from automatic learning.
   TypeScript checks, production builds and all 210 Node tests passed.
